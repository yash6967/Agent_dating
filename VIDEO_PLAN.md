# Three-minute proof video

| Time | Recorded action | Proof the viewer should see |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Open the cohort and show 25 verified, public two-link entries. | Real names, LinkedIn and Instagram URLs, successful source status. |
| 0:20–0:45 | Paste a new pair of official links and start cohort analysis. | The background run, two source statuses, identity check, and last-checked timestamps. |
| 0:45–1:10 | Open the new profile page. | Needs, hobbies, interests, evidence links, and honest unknowns. |
| 1:10–2:05 | Start autonomous dating and watch a planned date unfold. | The chosen pair appears in the live plan; two agents exchange questions and responses with source keys. |
| 2:05–2:35 | Open the separate date evaluation and both people's rankings. | Explain what changed and why the two directional rankings differ. |
| 2:35–2:55 | Open refresh operations and another profile. | Daily due dates and versioned analysis. |

Record an actual app run. Keep the address bar or app title visible at the start, include the complete conversation rather than only a highlight montage, and end before three minutes. Do not present a rule-based fallback conversation as a model-powered date.
